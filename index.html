02 Who We Are

A single studio for the whole build.

YS began as two disciplines under one roof: a civil works crew that respects structure, and an interior studio obsessed with detail. Together we plan, build, and finish spaces without the usual hand-off between contractor and designer — so nothing gets lost in translation.

From soil report to soft furnishing, every stage is managed by people who were in the room for the last one.

In-house structural & design teams
