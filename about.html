01 Company Overview

Founded on-site, not in a showroom.

YS interior was founded in 2012 by a civil engineer and an interior designer who kept running into the same problem: contractors and designers who never talked to each other, leaving clients to manage the gap themselves.

Today we run both disciplines under one studio — structural teams and design teams working from the same drawing set, the same budget, and the same deadline.

Our Vision

To be the studio Pune trusts to carry a space from bare land to finished room, with no compromise made between what stands up and what looks beautiful.

Our Mission

To deliver every project on a transparent budget and timeline, built by people who are accountable for both the structure and the space it becomes.
